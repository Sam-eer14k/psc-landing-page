3CX phone systems

3CX is phone system software. It runs on a standard PC or server in your office, so you own it instead of renting a box. Every extension, voicemail box and call queue lives there.

SIP trunking

A SIP trunk is an outside line delivered over your internet connection. You stop renting copper lines from the phone company, and calls between your own offices never touch their network.

  • Calls go out over the internet, not the phone company
  • Offices dial each other on three-digit extensions, free
  • Pacific Sailing pays $85 a month for six trunks

Hotel & PMS integration

The phone system talks to your property management system. Front desk checks a guest in and the room phone comes on. Housekeeping updates the room from the phone on the nightstand.

Field notes

Short notes on the gear questions we get asked most.

Networking2 min read

Why a PoE switch pays for itself

One switch powers every phone, plus paging, door phones and cameras.

Read the note

A Power over Ethernet switch powers each phone through its network cable. Put one battery backup next to the switch and every phone stays up when the power goes out. The same switch runs paging speakers, door phones, access points and cameras without extra outlets. Most phones need a separate power supply otherwise, so PoE often costs less. And because the switch is managed, we can restart every phone after an upgrade without visiting each desk.

Paging1 min read

One IP paging horn vs. a whole analog setup

Read the note

IP paging horns aren't cheap. But for one horn in a warehouse, or one per zone in a few zones, a 5-watt IP horn still costs less than an analog gateway, a page adapter, an amplifier and horns. It's tidier too. If you already have a legacy paging system or need several 70-watt horns in one spot, call us and we'll connect to what you have.

Phones1 min read

Why "cheap" phones online end up costing more

Read the note

Plug in an IP phone straight from an online store and it usually won't register. Someone has to update the firmware and enter your system's settings, and technicians bill $80 to $150 an hour for that. Our phones arrive upgraded and set up for your system, with a guide to the features you actually have. If one doesn't work when you plug it in, we make it work at no charge.
